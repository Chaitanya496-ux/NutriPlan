🌟 The "Why" Behind NutriPlan (Mission)
Most health apps are either too complex or too generic. NutriPlan is built on the philosophy of "Simplicity meets Intelligence." We believe that everyone deserves a personalized diet plan that respects their unique body type, cultural food preferences, and fitness goals.

🚧 The "Health Gap" (Problem Statement)
We identified three critical gaps in the current fitness industry:

The Information Paradox: Too much data (blogs, videos, influencers) leads to "Analysis Paralysis" for the average user.

The Static Trap: Traditional PDF diet charts don't adapt. If you miss a meal or change your workout, the chart becomes useless.

Accessibility Barrier: Personal nutritionists are expensive. NutriPlan brings that expertise to your screen for free.

🛠️ Tech Stack: The Engine Room
We chose a modern, scalable stack to ensure high performance:

Backend: Python 3.13 (The powerhouse for upcoming ML integration).

Framework: Flask (Micro-framework for rapid development and clean routing).

Security: Flask-WTF & CSRF Protection (Ensuring user data is never compromised).

Frontend: HTML5 & CSS3 (Custom UI designed for zero-clutter user experience).

Version Control: Git & GitHub (Continuous integration and collaboration).
